## Big Data Business Overview
### Business Categories
In 2023 Q1, I participated in the cloud migration project for big data business @ Sohu Intelligent Media R&D Center, from on-premise to Tencent Cloud. After the migration, we have reached the goal of reducing the cost and increasing operation & maintenance efficiency.

This article mainly shares: 
1. the big data business overview of our department (not confidential things included), 
2. the work and experience involved in the basic system, historical data, business system and other modules during cloud migration, 
3. the key technological reforms.
